# Market Context & Competitive Landscape — BizzAR
Source: Mordor Intelligence, Banuba, Blippar, search synthesis

## Digital Business Card Market
- **2026 size:** USD $217M
- **2031 projection:** USD $332M
- **CAGR:** 8.86% (2026–2031)
- **Fastest growing region:** Asia Pacific at 10.43% CAGR — our primary target market (India, SEA)
- **Largest market:** North America (35.17% of revenue)
- **Key growth drivers:** Hybrid work, sustainability mandates, smartphone penetration, contact-free networking
- **Platform breakdown:** iOS 46.23% share; progressive web apps fastest growing segment (9.11% CAGR)
- **Customer segment:** SME = 38.5% of market — our primary customer
- **Key players:** HiHello, Mobilo, CamCard, Popl, Blinq

## AR in Digital Business Cards
No major market report breaks out AR specifically. All reports focus on NFC/QR-based digital cards. AR business cards are an emerging premium segment within the broader market — no market size data exists yet.

**Inference:** AR is the differentiation play, not the core market. We're targeting the ~10-15% of digital card users who want a wow factor and are willing to pay a premium.

## BizzAR's Gap Analysis
Nobody offers:
1. Physical card image tracking (not QR-based) + 
2. Drag-drop module editor (vCard, socials, video, etc.) + 
3. Freemium self-serve SaaS + 
4. Mobile browser, no app install

This combination is the product.

## Pricing Strategy (from design doc)
- **Free:** 1 card, 2 panels (overlay + top), basic modules, BizzAR watermark
- **Pro:** Unlimited cards, all 4 panels, pro modules (video, GitHub, Calendly), no watermark
- Target price: $9-15/month Pro tier (compete with digital card SaaS, not enterprise AR)

## Growth Strategy
- Watermark on free tier = organic growth (every AR experience is an ad)
- Conference/networking scenarios = high virality (someone scans your card, asks "what is that?")
- QR code on physical card links to AR experience — no extra step for recipient
- Asia Pacific focus: large professional networking culture, high smartphone penetration, early adopter market
